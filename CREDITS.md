# Credits

pickle is built on [agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) by Addy Osmani, under the MIT license. Its skills, commands and agents are vendored: upstream text with local edits, synced by `scripts/vendor-sync.sh`. Each vendored skill folder carries the upstream `LICENSE`, [LICENSES/agent-skills.txt](LICENSES/agent-skills.txt) covers the commands and agents, and [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md) records the agent-skills version pickle is on.

`codebase-design` is ported, and `domain-modeling`, `setup` and `improve-codebase-architecture` rewritten for pickle, from [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) by Matt Pocock, under the MIT license ([LICENSES/mattpocock-skills.txt](LICENSES/mattpocock-skills.txt)). `reflect` also takes its environment lens from Matt's `retro`. [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md) records the version each was last compared against.

`typescript-best-practices`, `unslop`, `technical-writing`, `sharpen` and performance-optimization's `references/benchmark-checklist.md` are ported, and `how`, `why`, `blast-radius`, `reflect`, `teach` and codebase-design's `references/sketch-first.md` (from `architect`) and the subagent brief in `references/orchestration-patterns.md` (from poteto-mode) rewritten for Claude Code, from [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) by Lauren Tan ([@poteto](https://github.com/poteto)), published by Cursor in [cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins) under the MIT license. [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md) records the pstack version each was last compared against.
