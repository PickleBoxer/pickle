# Incident and postmortem context

Add these queries to your own source when the target looks defensive. Stay inside your source.

- **Issue / ticket tracker**: tickets labeled `incident`, `sev-*`, `postmortem-action-item` or `reliability` around the date the target was added.
- **Real-time team chat**: incident and severity channels (often `#incident-*`, `#sev-*`) around the same dates.
- **Long-form documents**: postmortems that name the target's file, symbol or error string.
- **Source control history**: commit messages like "fix for incident", "add defensive check", or a "revert" followed by "re-apply with...".
